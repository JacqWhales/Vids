# Never Have I Ever: Summit Edition — Script v2

Rewrite pass. Faster open, more fighting between the leaders, running gags that pay off,
and a real third-act blow-up before Gary's speech. **NEW** and **CHANGED** mark what's different
from v1; anything unmarked is kept. Target runtime is about the same (~4 min).

**Running gags this time**
- **The tea trolley** shows up three times. Gary 1 says no. Gary 2 says yes. Gary 3 says no, very fast.
- **Gary keeps getting replaced.** Gary 1, then Gary 2, then Gary 3, who wears a bike helmet.
- **Trump keeps putting his fingers back up** every time he loses them.
- **The red buttons.** Trump's Diet Coke button sets up the nuclear standoff at the end.

---

## COLD OPEN — CHANGED (gets to the first joke in about 5 seconds)

*No silent establishing shot. The caption "GENEVA — A TOP SECRET SUMMIT" types on while Gary is already talking.*

**GARY:** ...so the trade talks are cancelled, the climate talks are cancelled, and the hostage exchange is "pending." So! Party game.

**GARY:** Never Have I Ever. Ten fingers up. If you've done it, finger down.

*Everyone's hands pop up. Trump's hands are invisible from this distance.*

**GARY:** Mister President, I can't see your fingers.

*SMASH ZOOM on Trump's tiny hands. A tiny twinkle.*

**TRUMP:** They're there. Tremendous fingers. Doctors look at these fingers and they cry.

## ROUND 1 — Trump

**TRUMP:** Never have I ever been convicted of a felony.

*Beat. He looks down.*

**TRUMP:** ...Oh.

*FELONY COUNTS counter: 1, 2, 3... all of his fingers go down by 10, and it keeps counting to 34. GUILTY stamp.*

**TRUMP:** Kim, can I borrow some fingers?

**KIM:** No.

**TRUMP:** Witch hunt. I'm putting them back up.

*All ten fingers pop back up. A pop sound.*

**GARY:** You can't just—

**TRUMP:** Back up. Totally exonerated.

**PUTIN:** *(genuinely curious)* Your judges... they are still alive? — **CHANGED**

**XI:** In China we have ninety-nine point nine percent conviction rate. Congratulations, Donald. You are basically Chinese now. — **CHANGED**

**KIM:** In my country, the judge would be very sorry. And so would his grandparents. — **CHANGED**

---

## ROUND 2 — Kim (kept, slightly tighter)

**KIM:** Never have I ever used nerve agent in an airport.

*Kim puts his own finger down, proudly.*

**GARY:** Sir, you're supposed to say something you HAVEN'T done.

**KIM:** I know. I am bragging.

*Everyone slowly turns to Putin.*

**PUTIN:** Does airplane count?

**KIM:** Close enough, comrade.

*Finger down. HIGH FIVE. SMACK!*

---

## ROUND 3 — Putin

**PUTIN:** Never have I ever had critic fall out of window.

*Everyone turns to the window. A man in a suit falls past, screaming.*

**PUTIN:** Wind.

*Beat. A SECOND man falls past.* — **NEW**

**PUTIN:** ...Very windy day.

**GARY:** Shouldn't you put a finger d—

*Squeak, squeak. The tea trolley rolls in and stops at Gary. The waiter holds out a cup.*

**GARY:** I'm good! I'm so good. Thank you.

*The trolley squeaks away.*

---

## ROUND 4 — Trump vs. Putin — NEW

**TRUMP:** Never have I ever been controlled by Vladimir Putin.

*Silence. Putin calmly reaches over and pushes one of Trump's tiny fingers down for him. It's a tiny boop.*

**TRUMP:** ...That was a reflex.

**PUTIN:** *(pats his hand)* Good boy. — *(optional: cut this if it's too much)*

**TRUMP:** I'm putting it back up.

---

## ROUND 5 — Xi (kept)

**XI:** Never have I ever heard of June fourth, nineteen eighty-nine.

*Putin, Kim and Trump each put a finger down. Xi keeps all ten up and smiles. Slow push in on Xi.*

**TRUMP:** Oh, come on, the tank guy! Everybody knows the tank gu—

*HARD CUT: "This content is not available in your region." Error code: 6-4-1989.*

*Back in the room. Trump blinks.*

**TRUMP:** What was I saying?

**GARY:** Mister Xi, has anyone ever told you, you look a little like Winnie the—

*CENSORED. BEEP. When the picture comes back, Gary is a different man in a green shirt. His badge says GARY 2.*

**GARY 2:** Hi! I'm the new Gary. Love the honey. — **CHANGED** *(Xi's smile stays exactly the same. The honey pot quietly slides out of shot.)*

---

## THE BUTTON — NEW

*Trump casually reaches for a big red button on the table in front of him.*

**GARY 2:** Sir, what is th—

*Trump presses it. A loud BZZT.*

*PANIC. Kim dives under the table. The bear covers its eyes with its paws. Putin doesn't move. Xi sips his tea.*

*The waiter wheels in the trolley. There's a single Diet Coke on a silver tray.*

**TRUMP:** Diet Coke. Relax.

*Kim slowly climbs back up from under the table.*

**KIM:** Your button... is for Diet Coke?

**TRUMP:** Mine is. What's yours for?

*Kim looks at the red button in front of him. He slowly takes his hand off it.*

**WAITER:** *(to Gary 2)* Tea, sir?

**GARY 2:** Oh! Sure, thanks!

*He takes a sip. Everyone watches him in total silence. CUT TO BLACK, one second.*

*Back in: Gary is a new, shakier man in a bike helmet. His badge says GARY 3.*

**GARY 3:** I'm Gary. Please don't ask about the other Garys.

---

## ROUND 6 — Kim, the love letters

**KIM:** Never have I ever written love letters to another world leader.

*Kim slowly puts a finger down, looking at Trump. The sax music starts and everything goes pink with hearts. Trump puts a finger down too.*

**TRUMP:** He wrote me beautiful letters. We fell in love.

**KIM:** You don't write anymore.

**TRUMP:** I've been busy, Kim. There's tariffs.

**PUTIN:** He writes to me also.

*RECORD SCRATCH. The pink is gone.*

**XI:** He sends me only tariffs. — **NEW**

**KIM:** DONALD!

**TRUMP:** It's a very different relationship. Very different. All of them. Different.

---

## ROUND 7 — Trump, the Nobel (tighter)

**TRUMP:** Never have I ever been robbed of a Nobel Peace Prize.

*Nobody moves. Trump slams his own finger down. The table shakes.*

**TRUMP:** Rigged! I ended eight wars! Nine! Some were very small!

*Obama glides past the window, wearing his medal. Twinkle.*

**TRUMP:** Oh, come ON.

---

## ROUND 8 — Term limits, then the blow-up — CHANGED

**GARY 3:** Final round. Never have I ever stayed in power past my term limit.

**PUTIN:** I changed constitution. Terms reset. Very legal.

**XI:** We removed term limits in 2018. Much more efficient.

**KIM:** What is... term limit?

*Close on Trump, who silently puts on a red TRUMP 2028 hat. Pop.*

**GARY 3:** So nobody puts a finger down... so nobody wins?

*Beat. Then all four at once:*

**ALL FOUR:** I WIN.

*The argument escalates fast, with quick cuts between the four of them:*

**TRUMP:** I have the most fingers. I put them back up. Four times. A record.

**PUTIN:** Is not who has fingers. Is who counts fingers.

**XI:** History will say I won. We write the history.

**KIM:** I win. Also, I invented this game. And fingers.

*Each leader slowly puts a hand on their own red button. Western standoff: whistling music, extreme close-ups on eyes, the bear sweating, the clock ticking loud.*

*(Trump's button is still the Diet Coke button. He doesn't seem to know that.)*

**GARY 3:** WAIT!

---

## THE SPEECH — kept, re-pointed

*The soft guitar music starts. Close on Gary 3, helmet and all.*

**GARY 3:** You know... I learned something today.

**GARY 3:** We came here to talk about trade, and nuclear weapons, and human rights.

**GARY 3:** But maybe the real weapons of mass destruction... were the friends we made along the way.

*Long silence. One by one, the leaders take their hands off their buttons.*

*Then all four slide their teacups toward Gary at the same time.*

**GARY 3:** *(instantly)* I'm good.
